About CloudCerts

Your compass for navigating cloud certifications.

CloudCerts is a free, vendor-neutral guide that helps you find the right cloud certification path across Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services, and Google Cloud. Instead of guessing where to begin, you tell us your experience and career focus, and we map a clear, ordered roadmap from foundational to expert credentials.

Every certification in the catalog includes the skills tested, prerequisites, exam details, real-world job roles, and what to study next — all in one place. Your progress is saved right in your browser, so you can plan, track, and complete your journey at your own pace.

How to Use CloudCerts

01

Pick your starting point

On the homepage, select your experience level, focus area, and preferred provider to get an instant recommendation.

02

Build your pathway

Head to the Pathway page for an interactive roadmap. Click any node to see details and mark it Planned, In Progress, or Completed.

03

Explore the details

Open any certification to dig into skills, exam format, costs, and the career roles it unlocks.

04

Track and conquer

Your progress is saved locally. Come back anytime to update your status and plan your next certification.
